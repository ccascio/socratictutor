# Product Brief — AI Socratic Learning Studio

## Positioning

**For:** Professionals who need deep conceptual understanding of AI/ML — not a tutorial, not a course, not a chatbot.

**What it is:** A Socratic AI tutor that challenges your reasoning, detects your misconceptions, and builds a persistent personal knowledge base as you learn.

**What it is not:** A question-answering tool. An answer is not understanding.

---

## Core Promise

> "Understand deeply, don't just get answers."

Every session ends with structured knowledge — not a chat log. The user walks away with concepts mapped, misconceptions logged, flashcards generated, and a personal knowledge graph that grows session over session.

---

## Initial Vertical

**AI/ML for Professionals**

Target: software engineers, product managers, founders, and technical leads who work *with* AI systems but have gaps in conceptual understanding. They know how to call an API; they don't know why attention works.

Examples of topics: embeddings, transformers, attention mechanisms, fine-tuning vs. in-context learning, RLHF, vector databases, RAG architecture, gradient descent intuition.

Secondary vertical (future): physics/cosmology. Excluded from Phase 0 scope.

---

## Primary Differentiators

**1. Misconception Tracking** — the system does not just record what you studied; it records what you misunderstood. Each misconception is stored, linked to the session where it appeared, and resurfaces in review until resolved. This is the feature competitors don't have.

**2. Structured Knowledge Output** — every session generates a machine-readable artifact: summary, concepts extracted, misconceptions flagged, flashcards, quiz questions, and suggested next lesson. Chat history is a side effect, not the product.

**3. Persistent Learner Profile** — the tutor gets smarter the more you use it. It knows your preferred analogies, your recurring error patterns, your depth level, and which concepts you've truly mastered vs. passed quickly. This profile is the moat.

**4. Guided, Not Lecturing** — the LLM is constrained to ask questions, not explain. It gives a minimal explanation only after the user has exposed their reasoning. This forces active recall and surfaces real gaps.

---

## Success Metrics (Phase 1 target)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Sessions with ≥1 misconception detected | > 60% |
| Users returning for a second session | > 40% |
| Session artifacts exported | > 20% of completed sessions |
| Average concepts extracted per session | 3–7 |

- Knowledge graph visualization
- Export (Markdown, Anki, Obsidian)
- Auth / user accounts
- Monetization
